## Brand & Style

This design system encapsulates a premium, authoritative, and contemporary UK travel agency. It pairs the dependable gravitas of classic British touring heritage with the tactile slickness of a modern booking engine.

### Emotional Target & Voice
The emotional tone balances aspirational discovery with grounded certainty. Travellers should perceive the platform as curated, reliable, prestigious, and calm. The voice avoids hyperbole or discount-aggregator clutter, prioritizing editorial photography, clear pricing structures, and effortless transaction flows.

### Design Movement: Modern Heritage Minimalism
The aesthetic bridges restrained British editorial design with contemporary digital product architecture. It utilizes:
- Deep, prestige hues paired with warm mineral metallics for high-conversion highlights.
- Generous structural whitespace and fine architectural grid divisions.
- Crisp, low-contrast structural borders to anchor photography without visual noise.
- Tactile, deliberate interactive states that communicate security throughout the multi-step checkout and split-payment schemes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate standard of prestige and financial confidence:

- **Primary (`#00B4D8` - Bright Ocean Cyan):** Anchors main navigation bars, primary structural headers, hero banners, and brand recognition points. Represents security and luxury.
- **Secondary (`#0A1628` - Deep Navy):** Serves as high-contrast body typography, dark UI footers, modal headers, and dense tabular data borders.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB703` - Warm Amber Gold):** Reserved explicitly as the high-conversion accent. Governs primary booking CTAs ("Book Now", "Pay Now"), active radio card badges, selected states, and holiday rating stars. Never dilute with excessive decorative use.
- **Neutral (`#F8F9FA` - Off-White Neutral):** Controls secondary text labels, inclusion icons, and breadcrumb navigation.
- **Semantic & Surface Accents:** Canvas relies on pure whites (`#FFFFFF`) layered on tinted chalk foundations (`#FAFAFA` and `#F3F5F7`). Feedback states (Success, Warning, Error) utilize low-saturation, balanced UK institutional signaling.

## Typography

The type system blends classical high-editorial authority with functional tabular and interface legibility:

- **Playfair Display (Display & Major Headlines):** Used for primary section titles, destination feature banners, hero hooks, and editorial blog titles. Injects warmth, tradition, and bespoke luxury.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Interface, Body, and Conversion Data):** A geometric, modern sans-serif optimized for clear optical scanning of complex pricing tiers, travel durations, itinerary timelines, and form validations.
- **Price Figures:** Prices and total figures always utilize `Plus Jakarta Sans` with semi-bold or bold weights and tabular lining numerals to ensure exact horizontal visual rhythm across calculation breakdowns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a standard 12-column fluid grid on desktop, scaling into an 8-column system on tablet and a single-column or 2-column stacked flow on mobile.

### Canvas & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px - 1440px max-width container):** Standard 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` outer margins. Filter sidebar occupies 3 columns; listing package grid spans 9 columns (3-up card layout).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters. Filter sidebar collapses into a slide-over trigger sheet. Holiday cards drop to a 2-up grid.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer screen padding. Booking actions lock to a persistent sticky bottom navigation bar.

### Spacing Principles
- **Editorial Breathing Room:** Hero blocks and narrative highlights adopt `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` vertical padding.
- **Card Clustering:** Component interiors utilize tight, structured spacing (`space-md` between meta items; `space-lg` card edge padding) to maintain immediate visual coherence.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud, artificial Drop Shadows or aggressive floating effects. Instead, it creates depth through refined tonal surfaces, micro-elevation, and crisp hairline framing:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8F9FA`
  - Cards, panels, and dropdowns: `#FFFFFF`
  - Sticky bottom sheets and flight/hotel summary modules: `#FFFFFF`
- **Hairline Framing:** Cards and form containers rely primarily on 1px solid `#E2E8F0` borders rather than thick boundaries.
- **Restrained Shadows:**
  - *Standard Card:* `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.04), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.02)`
  - *Hover State:* Subtle displacement with `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.04)`
  - *Floating Booking Summary / Sticky Headers:* `0 -4px 16px 0 rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.06)` for bottom panels, accompanied by a clean `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Overlay & Backdrop:** Modal sheets, search filters, and lightboxes deploy a deep slate scrim: `rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.6)` with a light `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape language:
- Standard UI interactive components (buttons, input fields, dropdown triggers, and select boxes) utilize `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) corner radiuses.
- Content cards, destination image masks, and itinerary step containers utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Modals, flyout filter sheets, and major hero card containers use `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Pill elements (`rounded-full`) are strictly preserved for status chips, duration badges, and category labels. Overly bulbous or rounded containers are prohibited to maintain a sharp, professional UK travel agency identity.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Warm Amber Gold (`#FFB703`) background with Dark Navy or Bright Ocean Cyan text, bold font weight, `rounded-md`, and smooth `background-color` transitions on hover. Used for "Book Now", "Pay Now", and search actions.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1.5px solid Primary border and Primary text. Hovers into a light ocean wash.
- **Destructive/Ghost:** Minimal padding, zero shadow, slate text transitioning to high-contrast red on hover.

### 2. Holiday & Destination Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white background with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `0.5rem` radius.
- **Visuals:** Top destination image pinned to a strict 16:10 or 4:3 aspect ratio with `object-cover`.
- **Badges:** Overlaid tags positioned with `top: 12px; left: 12px` using semi-translucent dark slate or crisp white pills with dark text.
- **Pricing Unit:** Displayed in bottom-right layout: small uppercase "From" muted text stacked above large bold price figures (`price-display`), aligned adjacent to the secondary or primary "View Details" trigger.

### 3. Payment Scheme Selection Cards
- **Interactive Radio Cards:** Grid of selectable tiles representing payment options (e.g., Full Payment vs. 1st Part Payment).
- **Default State:** 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, white surface, radio indicator unoccupied.
- **Selected State:** 2px solid `#FFB703`, background tinted appropriately, checked accent indicator, and clear dynamic breakdown of "Payable Today" vs "Remaining Balance".

### 4. Form Controls & Inputs
- **Inputs & Selects:** Height standard of 44px (touch-friendly), 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, internal horizontal padding of `space-md`, focus ring with 2px offset.
- **Traveller Counter:** Stepper control unit with clear circular `+` and `-` buttons bounding a tabular counter figure. Minimum tap targets of 40x40px.

### 5. Itinerary Timeline
- Vertical progress trace in 2px `#E2E8F0`.
- Day markers defined by 24px circles filled with Primary color containing white day indices, accompanied by expandable accordion panels describing daily city tours, inclusions, and transport.

### 6. Badges & Chips
- **Status Indicators:** Pills with light background tints and saturated text labels (`#EBF8EE` with `#1E7E34` for Confirmed, `#FEFCBF` with `#B7791F` for Pending).
- All badges enforce uppercase tracking (`label-sm`).
